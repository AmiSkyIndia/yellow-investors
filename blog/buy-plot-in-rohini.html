Buy Plot in Rohini, Delhi: A Complete Guide for First-Time and Repeat Buyers

By Yellow Investors & Developers · Updated 03 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

If you're searching to buy a plot in Rohini, you're looking at one of Delhi's most reliable property investments — DDA approved land, clean titles and a planned sub-city that keeps appreciating. Here's what to check before you buy, and where to find verified plots.

Why Buy a Plot in Rohini Instead of a Flat

A plot gives you something a flat never will — control. You decide when to build, how to build and how large to build, and in Rohini's case, you're doing it on DDA approved land with a sector grid, wide roads and green belts already in place. Unlike unauthorised colonies elsewhere in Delhi NCR, a Rohini plot comes with a bank-recognised title, which means faster loan approvals and far fewer resale headaches down the line.

What to Check Before You Buy a Plot in Rohini

DDA Approval & Clear Title

Always confirm the plot is DDA approved with a clean chain of title. This single check protects you from disputes and is what banks look for before sanctioning a loan.

Plot Size & Use (Residential vs Commercial)

Rohini plots range from compact 26–32 sq. mtr. sizes to larger 209 and 335 sq. mtr. plots. Decide upfront whether you need a residential or commercial plot, since zoning differs by sector.

Financing & Budget

Banks typically finance up to 70% of the plot value for DDA approved land in Rohini, with approvals in as little as 15 days. Know your budget and loan eligibility before you shortlist plots.

Location Within Rohini (Sector 27–38)

Sectors closer to metro stations, main roads and upcoming infrastructure like Sector 34's sports complex and amusement park tend to see faster appreciation than interior pockets.

Where to Buy: Rohini Sector 34 is Worth a Look

If you're deciding where in Rohini to buy, Sector 34 is currently drawing the most fresh investment — a planned DDA Sports Complex, an amusement park, a dedicated cycle track to Sector 35, and a future Phase IV metro station are all lined up here. Plot sizes in Sector 34 run from 26 and 32 sq. mtr. up to 209 and 335 sq. mtr., covering both first-time buyers and larger investors.
