Why Buying a Plot in Rohini is One of Delhi's Smartest Property Decisions

By Yellow Investors & Developers · Updated 27 Sept 2026 · 6 min read

Rohini isn't just another locality on the map of Delhi — it's one of the capital's largest planned residential sub-cities, built ground-up by the DDA with wide roads, green belts and room to grow. Here's the full picture of why plot buyers keep coming back to Rohini Sector 27 to 38, and why Sector 34 in particular is drawing fresh attention right now.

Rohini: A Planned Sub-City, Not an Accident

Most of Delhi grew organically — narrow lanes retrofitted with traffic, drainage and parking after the fact. Rohini was different. Conceived by the Delhi Development Authority in the 1980s as one of its flagship planned sub-cities, it was laid out sector-by-sector with roads, sewage, parks and community facilities designed in before the plots were sold, not after. That single decision four decades ago is the reason Rohini still has wide carriageways, defined green belts and a legible sector grid today, at a scale few other parts of Delhi can match.

For a plot buyer, that history matters directly: you're not just buying land, you're buying into infrastructure that was engineered to support decades of growth — something that's very hard to retrofit into an already-built, congested locality.

The Core Reasons Buyers Prefer Rohini

Strong & Growing Metro Connectivity

The Red Line already runs through Rohini East, Rohini West and Rithala. The upcoming Rithala–Narela–Nahapur Phase IV corridor, with a planned station in Sector 34, will push connectivity even deeper into the newer sectors.

Direct Access to Major Road Corridors

Rohini sits close to the Ring Road, NH-44 (GT Karnal Road) and Outer Ring Road, with the under-development UER-II expressway set to cut travel time to Gurugram, Dwarka and the airport corridor.

Genuine DDA-Approved Title

Every plot is verified and DDA approved, which means clean, bank-recognised title documents — the single biggest factor that keeps home loan approvals fast and resale disputes rare.

Easy Financing

Because the title is clear, banks routinely finance up to 70% of the plot value, with loan approvals in as little as 15 days — something far harder to secure on unauthorised or disputed land elsewhere in NCR.

Room to Build the Way You Want

Unlike a flat, a plot lets you design and construct on your own timeline — and Rohini's Joda plot option lets joint families combine adjoining plots for a larger, single build.

Established Social Infrastructure

Reputed schools, multi-specialty hospitals, Metro Walk & Aggarwal City malls, and the Rohini district courts already anchor daily life — buyers aren't waiting for a locality to "develop", it already has.

Land Supply is Limited — Rohini's Isn't Getting Any Bigger

Delhi has almost no vacant land left for large-scale new plotted development. Rohini was one of the last sub-cities where the DDA could plan and release residential and commercial plots at scale, across Sectors 27 to 38. With very little comparable land supply remaining anywhere in the capital, existing Rohini plots carry a scarcity value that plays out as steady, long-term price appreciation — especially as the surrounding infrastructure (metro, roads, amenities) continues to mature.

Rohini Sector 34: The Corridor Getting the Freshest Investment

Within Rohini, Sector 34 has become the area to watch. It's slated to get a DDA Sports Complex spanning 22.25 hectares, a DDA-approved amusement park across 54.7 acres, and a dedicated 30m-wide cycle track connecting Sectors 34 and 35. Combined with the planned Phase IV metro station, these projects are exactly the kind of civic investment that historically precedes a fresh wave of appreciation in Rohini's older sectors too.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to buy a DDA approved plot in Rohini?

Yes. DDA approval means the plot has a clear, government-recognised title, which is exactly what banks check before sanctioning a home loan, and what protects you from ownership disputes later.

Why is Rohini considered better planned than other Delhi localities?

Because it was designed sector-wise from the outset by the DDA in the 1980s, with roads, drainage, parks and community space planned before plots were even sold — rather than added piecemeal to an already-built area.

Can I get a loan against a Rohini plot?

Yes, banks typically finance up to 70% of the plot value for DDA approved properties in Rohini, with approvals possible within about 15 days.

What is a Joda plot?

A Joda plot is two adjoining plots sold and combined together, giving joint families or larger builds more space and flexibility than a single standard-size plot.
