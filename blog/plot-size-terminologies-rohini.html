Plot Size Terminologies in Rohini, Delhi Explained

By Yellow Investors & Developers · Updated 09 Oct 2026 · 5 min read

Walk into any Rohini plot deal and you'll hear terms like "Joda plot," "corner plot" or "209 sq. mtr." thrown around as if everyone already knows what they mean. Here's a plain-language breakdown of every plot size term you'll come across, so you can shop with confidence.

Why Plot Size Terminology Matters in Rohini

Rohini was developed by the DDA as a planned sub-city, which means every plot was allotted against a fixed, documented size and category. Unlike unauthorised colonies where sizes can be vague or disputed, a Rohini plot's size and category are part of its official record. Knowing the terminology helps you compare listings accurately, estimate construction potential, and avoid being misquoted on price per unit area.

Common Plot Size Terms You'll Hear in Rohini

Sq. Mtr. vs Sq. Yard

DDA records plot sizes in square metres (sq. mtr.), but many buyers and brokers still quote in square yards out of habit. 1 sq. yard is roughly 0.836 sq. mtr., so a "100 sq. yard" plot is close to 84 sq. mtr. Always confirm which unit a listing is using before comparing prices.

Standard DDA Plot Categories

Rohini residential plots were allotted in fixed size bands — commonly 26, 32, 50, 60, 90, 120, 180, 209 and 335 sq. mtr. Each band corresponds to a category used in DDA's original allotment scheme, and resale listings still reference these standard sizes.

Joda Plot

A "Joda plot" (literally "pair plot") means two adjoining plots sold or merged together as one larger parcel. Joda plots are popular with buyers who want a bigger footprint than a single standard allotment allows, often for a larger independent house or a commercial build.

Corner Plot

A corner plot faces two roads instead of one, usually commanding a premium of 10–15% over an identically sized interior plot. The extra road frontage means more natural light, easier vehicle access and, for commercial plots, better visibility.

Other Terms That Affect Plot Size and Usability

Front-facing width (frontage): Even plots of the same area can have different frontage-to-depth ratios. A wider frontage is generally preferred since it affects how the house or shop can be designed, and it's a key factor DDA used when drawing up sector layouts.

FAR (Floor Area Ratio) / permissible built-up area: Plot size tells you the land area, but FAR tells you how much you're allowed to construct across all floors. Rohini's building bye-laws specify FAR by plot category, so a larger plot size usually — but not always — means more built-up area.

Residential vs Commercial plot: The same size terminology (sq. mtr., corner, Joda) applies across both categories, but zoning differs by sector and determines what you can legally build or run on the plot.

Quick Reference: Approximate Sq. Mtr. to Sq. Yard

26 sq. mtr.

Approximately 31 sq. yards — among the smallest standard DDA residential plot sizes in Rohini.

32 sq. mtr.

Approximately 38 sq. yards — a common entry-level plot size for first-time buyers.

90 & 120 sq. mtr.

Approximately 108 and 144 sq. yards respectively — mid-range sizes suited to independent houses.

209 & 335 sq. mtr.

Approximately 250 and 400 sq. yards respectively — larger plots popular with investors and bigger residential builds, especially in sectors like Rohini Sector 34.

Don't Let Terminology Confuse Your Decision

These terms exist to make plot comparison easier once you know them, but they can just as easily be used to create confusion if you're dealing with someone who isn't upfront about what a listing actually means. The safest way to navigate this is to work with a dealer who explains every term in writing and backs it up with the plot's official DDA record.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Joda plot in Rohini?

A Joda plot is two adjoining plots combined and sold as a single larger parcel, giving buyers more land than a standard single allotment.

Are Rohini plot sizes measured in sq. yards or sq. mtr.?

Official DDA records use square metres. Sq. yards are commonly used in conversation and some listings, so always confirm the unit before comparing prices.

Does a corner plot cost more than a regular plot of the same size?

Yes, corner plots typically command a 10–15% premium due to dual road frontage, better light and easier access.

How do I know the exact size category of a plot I'm interested in?

Ask for the plot's DDA allotment record, which specifies its official size in sq. mtr. and category. We verify this for every plot we list.
